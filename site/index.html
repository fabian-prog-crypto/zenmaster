Zen Master

You came for one video.

Zen Master removes the recommendation feeds from adult sites. The video you opened is the only one on the page.

Install for Chrome

The loop isn't you. It's the recommendation engine.

Adult sites run the same engine as every feed you can't put down, tuned for one thing: the next click. Related videos, up next, an endless grid under the player. It isn't there to help you find anything. It's there to keep you, and it works because it pulls on the same dopamine wiring in everyone.

What goes

  • Home page feeds
  • Related and recommended videos
  • Up next and end screens
  • Category, tag, model and channel listings
  • The automatic jump to the next video

What stays

  • Search, filters and results
  • The video you opened, and its player
  • Your history, favorites, subscriptions and playlists
Not a blocker.
It doesn't stop you from going to a site, doesn't judge you for going, and doesn't tell anyone you went. It removes the part of the page whose only job is to keep you there.
No off switch.
No pause, no reveal, no per-site disable. Bringing the feed back means removing the extension in Chrome's settings, which takes longer than an impulse lasts.
Nothing leaves your browser.
No account, no telemetry, no server. It never stores what you watched, what you searched for, or how much it hid.
Fifty sites, out of the box.
Pornhub, XVideos, XNXX and 47 more have built-in rules. Add any other site from the toolbar with one click, and Chrome grants access to that one address only.

Install for Chrome

Zen Master is free and isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet. Installing the current build takes about a minute.

  1. Download Zen Master 0.1.4 and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode in the top right.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and pick the folder you just unzipped.

It's on from that moment, on every site it covers, until you remove it.
